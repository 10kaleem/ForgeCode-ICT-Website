About us

Saeed Book Bank is an online book store. It describes itself as one of the largest online book stores and sells books on all topics at special prices.

What the store offers

  • New arrivals and best sellers every month
  • Books on many subjects
  • Special prices and discounts
  • Magazine subscriptions on the original website

About this website

This is a simpler, cleaner version of the Saeed Book Bank website. It has fewer menu links, clear prices in whole rupees and works on phones and computers. Orders are placed through WhatsApp, so there is no cart or login.

The site was designed by team ForgeCode as a course project for CSC-114 at Bahria University Islamabad. The book details and cover images come from the original store website.
